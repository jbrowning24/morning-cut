Privacy policy

Morning Cut. Last updated September 30, 2026.

Morning Cut is a personal tool used only by the person who built it. It is not offered to anyone else.

What it accesses

With its owner's permission, Morning Cut reads newsletters in the owner's Gmail inbox, sends one email a day to the owner's own address, and archives the newsletters it used. It does not delete email and does not read other people's accounts.

Where data goes

The program runs on the owner's computer, and its working files stay there. To sort and summarize the news, it sends newsletter and article text to two AI services: TypeSafe (Jev), which classifies each item, and Anthropic (Claude), which writes the briefing. Neither receives the owner's Gmail credentials.

What it doesn't do

Morning Cut does not sell data, show ads, share data with anyone else, or run on a public server.

Google user data

Morning Cut's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time from the Google Account's third-party access settings.
